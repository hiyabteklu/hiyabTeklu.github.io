How I Work

Four principles that have stayed consistent across every project, channel, and competition I've entered.

01
Principle 01
From the inside out
Most people research a topic then write about it. My starting point is different — I already understand the underlying system, whether that's a circuit, an algorithm, or a content architecture. Writing becomes the last step, not the first struggle. That gap is where the accuracy lives.
02
Principle 02
Reverse engineering everything
When something doesn't make sense, I don't move on. I take it apart until it does. This applies to electronics, software, client briefs, and unclear objectives equally. It's less a method and more a compulsion — I'm genuinely uncomfortable leaving things half-understood.
03
Principle 03
Psychological precision
I've spent years reading behavioral psychology — how people absorb information, where attention breaks, what creates trust, what kills it. That reading shapes every structural and word-level decision I make. Nothing in a finished piece is accidental. I know why each sentence is where it is.
04
Principle 04
Zero margin for error
I prepared 3,000+ questions for the Qesem Quiz App at 98% verified accuracy. Every derivation in every study guide published to 95,000+ students was solved by hand before release. The deadline is the latest acceptable point, not the target. I've never missed one — and I've beaten most of them by days.

Where I've Worked

Real roles. Real communities. Real accountability to students who were relying on the work being right.

Qesem Academy
Content Creator & Question Architect
95k+
High school students
The Situation
Ethiopian high school students were preparing for national entrance exams using study aids riddled with typos, wrong equations, and rounding errors. When a student trusts a wrong answer key and fails their exam, that's a year of their life lost.
What I Did
I volunteered to screen the syllabus files — and personally solved every one of 3,000+ chemistry, physics, and calculus questions by hand before they went live. I also created daily educational content across multiple subjects and managed community engagement across the channel.
The Cost
Weeks at my desk checking math sheets during my own exam periods. I knew if I got one derivation wrong, thousands of students would study the wrong answer. That knowledge made shortcuts impossible for me.
What It Built
A trusted, verified archive of step-by-step guides and a quiz app with 98% accuracy. The channel — @QesemAcademy — became the daily study resource for over 95,000 students and counting.
Wisdom Tower Network
Founder & Community Organizer
25k+
University students & researchers
The Situation
As engineering students in Ethiopia, finding textbooks, past exams, lab sheets, and reference material was genuinely difficult. Everything was scattered across slow links, private flash drives, and random folders nobody could find twice.
What I Did
Built and cataloged a free digital library from scratch. Spent weekends and holidays gathering over 10,000 items — PDF textbooks, past exam booklets, reference slides — and organized them into 100+ structured folders with clean naming and navigation.
The Cost
I paid for cloud storage personally and managed the entire community alone through my own midterms. I refused to run ads or charge access fees. The channel stayed free and completely focused on learning.
What It Built
@wisdom_tower1 now has over 25,000 active students, researchers, and young innovators sharing material and helping each other daily — no barriers, no cost, no ads.
