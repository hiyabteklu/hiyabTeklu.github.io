How I Work

Principles that shape every piece of work.

Principle 01
From the inside out
I do not research a topic then write about it. I already understand the underlying system. Writing becomes the last step, not the first struggle.
Principle 02
Reverse engineering everything
When something does not make sense, I do not move on. I take it apart until it does. It is less a method and more a compulsion. I am genuinely uncomfortable leaving things half-understood.
Principle 03
Psychological precision
I have spent years reading behavioral psychology — how people absorb information, where attention breaks, what creates trust, what kills it. That reading shapes every structural and word-level decision.
Principle 04
Zero margin for error
When I say I am accurate, I mean it. I am an engineer. I treat the work with the same precision as physics. I have never missed a deadline, and I usually beat them by days.

Where I Have Worked

Real roles. Real communities. Real accountability to students who were relying on the work being right.

Qesem Academy
Content Creator & Science Communicator
95k+ high school students
The Situation
High school students often struggle to find reliable information and verified resources for national entrance exams, exposing them to scams and low-quality materials scattered across private groups.
What I Did
I am a daily content creator at Qesem Academy and Qesem University. I solved over 3,000 entrance questions, prepared around 2,500 practice questions, and contributed more than 90% of their quiz app content.
The Cost
A lot of sleepless nights during my own exam periods, with the responsibility of thousands of students relying on my content and leaving no room for shortcuts.
What It Built
A trusted, verified archive of step-by-step guides and a quiz app with 99.8% accuracy. The channels became daily study resources for over 95,000 students and counting.
Wisdom Tower Network
Founder & Community Organizer
25k+ university students
The Situation
As a university student in Ethiopia, finding resources, advice, guides, and opportunities is genuinely difficult. Most students only have a rigid class schedule and little connection to real learning needs.
What I Did
Built and cataloged a free digital library from scratch. Gathered over 10,000 educational materials covering every course in Ethiopian higher education, plus time management tips, scholarships, and a space where students can find co-founders.
The Cost
I managed the entire community alone through my own midterms. I refused to run ads or charge access fees. The channel remained free and fully focused on learning.
What It Built
@wisdom_tower1 now has over 25,000 active students, researchers, and young innovators sharing materials and helping each other daily. No barriers, no cost, no ads.
