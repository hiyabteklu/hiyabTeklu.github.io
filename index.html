Track Record // Where I've Served

Qesem Academy

Math & Physics Checker
High School Study App // 95k+ Telegram Members
The Challenge

Ethiopian high school students were preparing for their national entrance exams, but many study aids online had typos, wrong equations, or rounding errors in their math charts. That is stressful when you are trying to study.

My Decision

I volunteered to screen the syllabus files. Out of over 3,000 chemistry, physics, and calculus assignment questions, I personally solved every single one by hand to make sure there weren't any mistakes in the calculations.

The Tradeoff

It took a huge amount of my free time and sat at my desk for weeks checking math sheets. It was tedious, but I knew if a high schooler gets a wrong answer key, they could fail their real test. I had to get it right.

What Changed

We built a solid, trusted collection of step-by-step study guides. Our channel @QesemAcademy grew to more than 95,000 students who rely on our homework keys and daily mock tests every day.

Wisdom Tower Network

Community Creator & Organizer
Free Student Resource Library // 25k+ Members
The Challenge

As engineering students, finding textbooks, past college exams, prep guides, and lab sheets in Ethiopia is incredibly hard. Everything was scattered across random, slow folders, old links, or kept in private flash drives.

My Decision

I wanted to fix this. I spent weekends and holidays gathering and cataloging over 10,000 digital items (PDF books, past exam sheets, reference slides) and sorted them cleanly into 100+ organized folders.

The Tradeoff

I had to pay for the cloud storage myself and managed the community alone during midterms. I refused to put ads or charge students to unlock guides, keeping it quiet, free, and completely focused on learning.

What Changed

It completely took off! Our channel @wisdom_tower1 now has over 25,000 active students and young researchers who help each other and share college files daily without barriers.

Science Communication & Writing

Science Writer
Simplifying Complex College Blueprints
The Challenge

Most physics papers, aerospace control loops, and AI code guides are written in the most complicated and boring way possible. It scares away beginners who feel like they aren't smart enough to grasp the science.

My Decision

I started writing visual articles that break down difficult concepts (like how satellite circuits operate or how basic IP network routing works) into friendly, human-scale explanations with simple charts.

The Tradeoff

It meant spending hours refining basic explanations and drawing neat, understandable diagrams rather than just working on my own private code projects.

What Changed

I proved that you don't need fancy terminology to explain engineering. Having students tell me they finally understand a complex topic without getting a headache is the most satisfying part of my work.
