How I Work

Four principles that have stayed consistent across every project, channel, and competition.

01
Principle 01
From the inside out
I don't research a topic then write about it. My starting point is different. I already understand the underlying system and the subject in hand. Writing becomes the last step, not the first struggle. That gap is where the accuracy lives.
02
Principle 02
Reverse engineering everything
When something doesn't make sense, I don't move on. I take it apart until it does. This applies to everything I've worked on equally. It's less a method and more a compulsion. I'm genuinely uncomfortable leaving things half-understood.
03
Principle 03
Psychological precision
I've spent years reading behavioral psychology. How people absorb information, where attention breaks, what creates trust, what kills it. That reading shapes every structural and word-level decision I make. I know why each sentence is where it is.
04
Principle 04
Zero margin for error
I solved 3,000 questions for a commercial quiz app at 99.8% verified accuracy. Verified and published daily educational content for hundreds of thousands of students. The deadline is the latest acceptable point, not the target. I've never missed one and I've beaten most of them by days.
