Track Record

Qesem Academy

Math & Physics Checker
High School Study Channel // 95k+ Telegram Members
The Problem

Students studying for the national university entrance exam were using materials with errors in them — wrong equations, incorrect rounding, typos in answer keys. That's a real problem when you're relying on them to pass.

What I Did

I went through over 3,000 chemistry, physics, and calculus problems and worked out each one by hand to catch mistakes before students used them.

The Cost

It took weeks of free time sitting at a desk going through worksheets. But a wrong answer key can cause someone to fail a high-stakes exam, so it had to be done carefully.

Result

We built a reliable set of step-by-step guides. @QesemAcademy grew to over 95,000 students who use the answer keys and daily mock tests regularly.

Wisdom Tower Network

Founder & Organizer
Free Study Library // 25k+ Members
The Problem

Finding engineering textbooks, past exams, and lab sheets in Ethiopia is genuinely difficult. Files were scattered across random links, private flash drives, or slow shared folders that were hard to navigate.

What I Did

I spent weekends collecting and sorting over 10,000 items — PDF textbooks, past exam sheets, reference slides — into organized folders that are easy to browse.

The Cost

I paid for cloud storage myself and ran it alone during exam seasons. No ads, no payment walls — I wanted it to stay useful and not get cluttered with monetization.

Result

@wisdom_tower1 now has over 25,000 active members who share files and help each other daily.

Science Writing

Science Writer
Simplifying Technical Topics for Students
The Problem

Most physics papers and technical guides are written in a way that makes beginners feel like they can't follow along, even when the underlying concept isn't that complicated.

What I Did

I started writing shorter explanations — how satellite circuits work, how IP routing works — in plain language with simple diagrams that don't require a background to understand.

The Cost

Simplifying something accurately takes more time than just writing technical language. It's not faster to write short and clear — it's slower. But it's more useful.

Result

Students have told me they finally understood a topic after reading one of my guides. That feedback is what makes it worth the time.

// What I chose to give up

Most weekends went into cataloging textbooks and checking solutions instead of other things. I kept the channels free and turned down ways to monetize them. The work was useful, so I kept doing it.

// A number I don't trust

Member counts. They're easy to inflate. The only feedback I trust is direct — a student saying a guide helped them pass their exam. That's the only metric that means anything to me.
