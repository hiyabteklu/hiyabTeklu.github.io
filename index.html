How I Work

Principles that shape every piece of work.

Principle 01
From the inside out
I don't research a topic then write about it. I already understand the underlying system and the subject in hand. Writing becomes the last step, not the first struggle.
Principle 02
Reverse engineering everything
When something doesn't make sense, I don't move on. I take it apart until it does. It's less a method and more a compulsion. I'm genuinely uncomfortable leaving things half-understood.
Principle 03
Psychological precision
I've spent years reading behavioral psychology. How people absorb information, where attention breaks, what creates trust, what kills it. That reading shapes every structural and word-level decision I make. I know why each sentence is where it is.
Principle 04
Zero margin for error
When I say I'm accurate, I mean it. I'm an engineer. I treat everything with the same unforgiving precision as physics. I've never missed a deadline, and I usually beat them by days.
